The “Butter Substitute” Riddle

The intended answer is the CSS margin property. The hint refers to margarine, a substitute for butter, because “margarine” sounds similar to “margin.”

How Margin Organizes Elements

Margin creates space outside an element’s border. By changing the top, right, bottom, and left margins, a designer can separate content and control the relationship between page areas without using relative or absolute positioning. Modern CSS Grid and Flexbox can also organize larger layouts, but margin is the answer connected to the lecture’s riddle.
