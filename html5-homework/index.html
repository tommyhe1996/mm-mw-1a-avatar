Building a Meaningful Web Page

HTML5 semantic elements give each part of a page a clear purpose. Instead of placing everything inside unnamed boxes, I can use header, nav, main, article, section, aside, and footer elements to describe how the information belongs together.

Structure Supports Creativity

A clear structure does not limit my creativity. It gives my ideas an organized place to grow. The header introduces my theme, the article contains the main story, and the sections divide that story into focused ideas that are easier to understand.

My Color Story

I selected deep teal and warm coral because they are complementary colors that create energy without copying the blue, pink, and peach palette from the class example. Teal represents calm focus and thoughtful design. Coral represents creative courage, optimism, and my willingness to experiment as I develop my future in multimedia and web design.
