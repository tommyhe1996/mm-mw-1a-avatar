Features I Observed

  • Differences between light and dark colors
  • Repeated colors, buttons, fonts, and shapes
  • Consistent alignment of text and images
  • Related information placed close together

My Design Analysis

Contrast — Apple MacBook Pro
I selected this page because its dark background contrasts strongly with the bright product images and white text. The contrast directs my attention toward the product and important information.
Repetition — California State Portal
I selected this site because it repeats colors, headings, icons, cards, and button styles. The repeated elements make the different sections feel connected and consistent.
Alignment — UC Berkeley
I selected this website because its headings, paragraphs, photographs, and navigation elements follow an organized grid. The alignment makes the information easier to read.
Proximity — City of Berkeley
I selected this site because related links and services are grouped close together. This helps visitors understand which pieces of information belong to the same category.
