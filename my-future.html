My Future: Designing My Own Life

My future is not one narrow job title. I see myself as a
multimedia web designer, artist, blogger, social influencer,
and running enthusiast.

I want to build a portfolio that connects technology with
art, wellness, psychology, and authentic self-expression.

I also want to help neurodivergent people practice designing
life on their own terms instead of following only one path.

My future goal is to create with courage, keep learning,
and make work that helps other people feel visible.
