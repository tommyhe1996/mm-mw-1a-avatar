My Creative Work

My creative work combines web design, multimedia, drawing,
video editing, personal storytelling, and life planning.

I want to design neurodivergent-friendly experiences that
are welcoming, understandable, and open to different ways
of thinking and expressing identity.

Running and making art help me develop ideas in my own way.
My goal is not to hide difference, but to make difference
part of the design.
